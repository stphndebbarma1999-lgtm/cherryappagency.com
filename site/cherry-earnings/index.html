Earnings & diamonds

Cherry App Earnings: Diamonds to USD Conversion

Cherry app earnings are counted in diamonds. The stated value is 10,000 diamonds = $1, so you can find the USD value of any balance by dividing it by 10,000. Use the table and converter below, then read the rates for how diamonds are earned.

Source: supplied Cherry recruiting policy. Content reviewed 29 October 2026.

100,000 diamonds ($10) is also the stated minimum withdrawal for hosts.

The formula

USD = diamonds ÷ 10,000

For example: 350,000 diamonds ÷ 10,000 = $35.

This is a mathematical conversion at the stated rate. It is not a statement of an approved payout, and we do not give INR conversions because we have not verified the exchange rate or method used for payment.

How hosts earn diamonds

These are the stated rates for Cherry host earnings. Actual earnings depend on how many paid calls and gifts a host receives, so they vary.

Read before using these figures

The policy's wording about the first 30 seconds of a video call and the second minute is unclear. We cannot confirm that every connected second or started minute earns the full video-call rate.

These per-minute figures are not an hourly, daily or monthly wage. Calls are not continuous or guaranteed, and nothing here predicts what any host will earn.

The salary plan described in a separate image ($21–$35 per week, with conditions) is explained on the Cherry host guide. We have not confirmed that the plan is currently active.

Agency earnings

Agencies do not earn diamonds per minute. They earn a weekly commission of 15%–40% on their hosts' eligible earnings from paid calls, gifts and text chat, depending on the tier reached. Commission is calculated on Mondays at 7:30 AM IST. The full table and its open questions are on the agency commission page.

Earnings questions

How much is 1 Cherry diamond worth?

One diamond is worth $0.0001, because 10,000 diamonds equal $1.

How many diamonds do I need to withdraw?

The stated host minimum is 100,000 diamonds, which is $10. See the Cherry withdrawal page for the review and payment process.

What is the value of Cherry diamonds in rupees?

We have not verified the exchange rate or conversion method used when payments are made in INR, so we don't publish a rupee figure. The policy states values in US dollars.
