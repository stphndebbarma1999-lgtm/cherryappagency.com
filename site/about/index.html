About

About Cherry App Agency

Cherry App Agency is an independent information and registration-enquiry website for people considering hosting on the Cherry app or running a Cherry agency. We explain the recruiting policy in plain language and handle enquiries on WhatsApp.

Content reviewed 29 October 2026.

Why this website exists

Recruiting information for the Cherry app often reaches people as PDFs, screenshots and forwarded messages. Those materials can be hard to read on a phone. Important conditions sit far from the headline figures, and some of the wording is unclear.

This website puts that information in one place. For hosts and agencies, each guide shows:

  • the figures as stated, in readable tables
  • the conditions attached to each figure
  • simple maths, such as converting diamonds to dollars, clearly labelled as maths
  • where the source is unclear, contradictory or silent

What we provide

  • Host guide: eligibility, earning rates, the salary-plan conditions and working rules
  • Agency guide: commission tiers, payment schedule and sub-agency rules
  • Earnings and withdrawal pages, with a diamond converter
  • A policy summary covering warnings and bans
  • Registration enquiries on WhatsApp at +91 9233262700

What we are not

We are not the publisher of the Cherry app and we do not claim an official partnership with it. Contacting us starts an enquiry. It does not create, submit or approve an account. We do not guarantee income, approval or payment timing. Those depend on the platform's own terms.

This website does not collect registration forms or identity documents, and it does not take payments.

Our sources

The content is based on, in order of priority:

  1. The Cherry recruiting policy document supplied to us
  2. A separately supplied salary-plan image, used only for the weekly salary details and always labelled as a separate source

Where those sources disagree with each other or are unclear, we say so beside the figure concerned. Each page shows the date its content was last reviewed. That date changes only when we actually review or edit the page.

Spotted an error?

If any information here is out of date or wrong, please tell us on WhatsApp and we will check it against the source.
