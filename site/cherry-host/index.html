Host guide

Cherry Host Registration and Earnings Guide

Cherry hosts earn diamonds from video calls, match calls and gifts, and can withdraw once they reach 100,000 diamonds ($10). The recruiting policy states that female hosts must be over 18. Read the rates, conditions and working rules below before you make an enquiry.

Cherry host eligibility

The supplied recruiting policy states: female hosts must be over 18.

That is the only eligibility requirement stated in the documents we have. Hosts must also follow the platform's conduct rules from their first call. Those rules include appearing alone on camera, keeping their face visible and never allowing anyone else to use their account.

We only handle enquiries from adults. If you are under 18, please do not contact us about hosting.

Confirm before applying

We have not confirmed the current in-app registration steps or any verification the platform requires. Ask us in your enquiry. This website never asks you to upload identity documents.

Video-call earnings

Video calls pay the highest stated rate: 1,000 diamonds per minute. For example, ten paid minutes at that rate would be 10,000 diamonds, or $1.

Unclear wording

The policy includes wording about the first 30 seconds of a call and about the second minute. Its meaning is unclear. We cannot confirm that every connected second, or every started minute, earns the full rate.

Match-call earnings

Match calls pay 500 diamonds per minute, half the video-call rate. That is $0.05 per minute at the stated conversion rate.

Gift share

When a user sends a gift, the host receives 70% of its value. A gift worth 10,000 diamonds, for example, would earn the host 7,000 diamonds ($0.70).

Convert any balance on the Cherry earnings and diamond conversion page.

Diamond conversion and minimum withdrawal

10,000 diamonds = $1. The stated host minimum withdrawal is 100,000 diamonds, which is $10.

Once your balance reaches the minimum, you can request a withdrawal. The policy says requests are reviewed within three working days, and payment follows approval. Bank transfer is listed as a method, and hosts are described as receiving their payments directly. Holidays can cause delays, and the review period is not a guaranteed arrival date. See the host withdrawal rules for the full picture.

Working guidelines for Cherry hosts

The policy lists behaviour that leads to warnings. Following these guidelines protects both your account and your earnings.

Do

  • Keep your face clearly visible on camera
  • Use enough lighting for you to be seen
  • Appear on camera alone
  • Stay attentive and awake during live sessions and calls
  • Focus on hosting during your working time

Avoid

  • Doing unrelated activities during working time
  • Having other people appear on camera, especially minors
  • Offensive language or actions
  • Any illegal drug-related activity
  • Sharing your account or obtaining diamonds by fraud

Serious violations (minors appearing on calls, illegal drug-related activity, offensive language or actions) are treated more strictly. The policy states that repeated serious-violation bans may result in a permanent ban.

Three actions lead to an immediate ban: letting another person use your host account, obtaining diamonds through fraud or illegal methods, and colluding in malicious refunds.

Cherry host FAQs

Tap "Enquire About Host Registration" on this page. WhatsApp opens with a message to +91 9233262700, which you can edit before sending. We will explain the current application steps. Sending a message does not create or approve an account.

What is the age requirement for Cherry hosts?

The supplied policy states that female hosts must be over 18. We do not accept enquiries from anyone under 18.

How much does a Cherry host earn per minute?

The stated rates are 1,000 diamonds ($0.10) per minute for video calls and 500 diamonds ($0.05) per minute for match calls, plus 70% of gift value. How the first part of a video call is counted is unclear. Actual earnings depend on how many paid calls you receive.

Is there a guaranteed salary for Cherry hosts?

No. A salary-plan image describes $21–$35 per week if strict conditions are met, but we have not confirmed that the plan is currently active. It is not guaranteed base pay.

What is the Cherry host minimum withdrawal?

100,000 diamonds, which is $10 at the stated conversion rate of 10,000 diamonds = $1.

Can someone else use my host account?

No. The policy lists letting another person use a host account as grounds for an immediate ban.
