What is the Cherry app?

The Cherry app recruiting materials describe a video-chat platform where users pay to talk with hosts. Hosts earn diamonds from video calls, match calls and gifts. Diamonds have a stated value of 10,000 diamonds = $1, and they can be withdrawn once a minimum balance is reached.

Alongside hosts, the platform runs an agency system. A Cherry agency brings hosts onto the platform and receives a weekly commission based on those hosts' earnings, using a tiered schedule from 15% to 40%.

This website is an independent guide to that policy. We set out what the recruiting documents say, show the figures in plain tables, and point out where the wording is unclear. If you decide to go ahead, you can make a registration enquiry with us on WhatsApp.

Cherry agency commission overview

The policy sets commission by tier. The rate depends on how much the agency's hosts earn in a week: 15% at the $300+ tier, rising to 40% at the $10,000+ tier. Most agencies will not be at the top rate, and nothing is stated for weeks below $300.

Diamond value and withdrawal

10,000 diamonds are worth $1. To find the dollar value of any balance, divide the number of diamonds by 10,000. For example, 250,000 diamonds is $25.

The stated host minimum withdrawal is 100,000 diamonds ($10). Hosts and agencies can request withdrawals. The policy says requests are reviewed within three working days, and payment follows approval. Bank transfer is listed as a payment method, and holidays can cause delays.

A review within three working days is not the same as money arriving in your bank in three days. We have not seen a guaranteed arrival time.

Policy highlights

The rules most likely to affect whether you can join and whether you get paid.

Adult eligibility

The policy states that female hosts must be over 18. Minors must never appear on calls or live sessions.

Account ownership

Only the registered host may use a host account. Letting another person use it is listed as grounds for an immediate ban.

Conduct on camera

Hosts must have their face visible and enough light, appear alone, and stay attentive. Warnings lead to restrictions or a ban.

Payment conditions

Diamonds gained through fraud or malicious refunds lead to a ban. Withdrawals are reviewed before payment is made.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the official Cherry App website?

No. Cherry App Agency is an independent information and registration-enquiry website. We are not the app's publisher, and we do not claim an official partnership. We summarise the recruiting policy supplied to us and point out where its wording is unclear.

Who can become a Cherry host?

The recruiting policy states that female hosts must be over 18. Hosts must also follow the platform's conduct rules and use their own account. Ask us to confirm the full current requirements before you apply. See the host eligibility details.

How much can a Cherry host earn?

There is no fixed income. Hosts earn 1,000 diamonds per minute on video calls (subject to unclear timing rules), 500 diamonds per minute on match calls and 70% of gift value. A conditional weekly salary of $21–$35 is described separately, but we have not confirmed that it is currently active.

What commission does a Cherry agency earn?

The policy sets commission from 15% to 40%, depending on the weekly earnings of the agency's hosts. The first listed tier is $300+ per week. The document's examples use 10%, so ask us to confirm the rate before you rely on it.

How many diamonds make one US dollar?

10,000 diamonds equal $1. Divide any diamond balance by 10,000 to get the USD equivalent. Try the diamond converter.

How long does a withdrawal take?

The policy says withdrawal requests are reviewed within three working days, and payment is made after approval. Holidays can cause delays. This does not guarantee when the money reaches your bank. More on the withdrawal page.

Does messaging you on WhatsApp register me?

No. A WhatsApp message starts an enquiry with us. It does not create, submit or approve an account. We never ask for identity documents through this website, and you should never share passwords or one-time codes with anyone.

Where can I download the Cherry app?

We have not yet verified the official download destination for this platform. Several unrelated apps use the name "Cherry", so we do not link to any app store listing or APK. Avoid installing APK files from unknown sources. See the app overview for the current status.
