Cherry App Withdrawal Rules and Payment Policy

Hosts and agencies can request withdrawals. The stated host minimum is $10 (100,000 diamonds). Requests are reviewed within three working days, and payment follows approval. A review within three working days is not a promise that money reaches your bank in three days.

Review first, then payment

A Cherry app withdrawal happens in two separate stages:

  1. Review. Your request is checked. The policy says this happens within three working days. Weekends and holidays are not working days, so a request made before a weekend or holiday can take longer.
  2. Payment. Once a request is approved, payment is made. The policy does not say how long a bank transfer takes after approval. Your bank's processing time is separate again.
Important distinction

"Reviewed within three working days" is not the same as "money in your bank within three days." We have not seen a guaranteed arrival date, and approval itself is not guaranteed.

Host and agency withdrawals

Hosts

Hosts can request a withdrawal once their balance reaches 100,000 diamonds ($10). The policy describes hosts as receiving their payments directly.

Host earnings and eligibility

Agencies

Agency commission is credited to the agency wallet every Monday. Agencies can then request a withdrawal, which follows the same review-then-payment process. We have not confirmed a separate minimum withdrawal for agencies.

What the policy does not tell us

The supplied documents don't answer the following questions, so we don't guess. Ask us to confirm any of them before you rely on a payment:

  • Whether any withdrawal fee or charge applies
  • Which exchange rate or deduction applies when paying into a non-USD account
  • Which identity or bank documents the platform asks for
  • Which countries and banks are supported
  • How long payment takes to arrive after approval
  • Whether payment methods other than bank transfer are offered
  • The minimum withdrawal for agency wallets

Withdrawal questions

What is the Cherry minimum withdrawal?

The stated host minimum is 100,000 diamonds, which is $10. A separate agency minimum has not been confirmed.

How long does a Cherry withdrawal take?

The policy says requests are reviewed within three working days, and payment follows approval. Holidays may cause delays. No bank arrival time is guaranteed.

How are Cherry payments made?

Bank transfer is the method listed in the policy. We have not confirmed whether other methods are available.

Do hosts get paid through their agency?

The policy describes hosts as receiving their payments directly.

Questions about a withdrawal?

Ask us about the review process or any of the points above that are not yet confirmed. If you have a problem with a specific account, also contact the platform through its own support channels.
